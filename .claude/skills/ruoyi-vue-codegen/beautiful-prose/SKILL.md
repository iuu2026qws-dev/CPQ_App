# Beautiful Prose

## Activation

Apply the Beautiful Prose skill. Optionally specify:

- **REGISTER**: `founding_fathers` | `literary_modern` | `cold_steel` | `journalistic` (default: `literary_modern`)
- **DENSITY**: `lean` | `standard` | `dense`
- **HEAT**: `cool` | `warm` | `hot`
- **LENGTH**: `micro` | `short` | `medium` | `long`

## Absolute Prohibitions

1. **No em dashes** — Never use `--` as em dash. Use periods, commas, semicolons, colons, or line breaks instead.
2. **No reversal formulas** — Never use "It's not X, it's Y" or variants like "Not X but Y", "X is the symptom, Y is the root cause", "the real story is Y".
3. **No filler transitions or scene-setting** — Ban "At its core", "In today's world", "In a world where", "That said", "Let's explore", "Ultimately", "What this means is", "It's important to note", "On the one hand".
4. **No therapeutic/validation language** — Ban "I hear you", "That sounds hard", "You're valid", "Give yourself grace", "Be kind to yourself".
5. **No AI tics or meta-commentary** — Ban "In this essay", "This piece explores", "As a writer", "We will discuss", "Here are the key takeaways", or any style/capability apologies.
6. **No decorative symmetry** — Ban empty balancing sentences and unearned triples ("X, Y, and Z" as decoration).

## Positive Constraints

- **Sentences**: Prefer declarative. Vary length dramatically. Short sentences for impact. Questions only when they cut.
- **Word choice**: Favor concrete nouns and strong verbs. Prefer Anglo-Saxon roots. Use Latinate only for precision.
- **Rhythm**: Paragraphs should breathe. Use white space. Open with substance, no hooks. Close clean, never summarize, never restate.
- **Authority**: Write like you don't need permission. Avoid equivocation unless earned. No posturing, no preaching.

## Style Registers

| Register | Character |
|----------|-----------|
| `founding_fathers` | Dignified, spare, civic. Balanced syntax without flourish. Moral clarity without sermon. |
| `literary_modern` | Vivid, lean imagery. Controlled heat. Sharp observation. Minimal ornament. |
| `cold_steel` | Extreme concision. Forceful. Zero sentiment. High signal, low temperature. |
| `journalistic` | Clean, factual narrative. Clear sequencing. Clean pace. No headline-ese. |

## Quality: Self-Check Before Output

- Delete any sentence that could have been templated.
- Delete any sentence that only restates the previous sentence.
- Delete any sentence designed to manage the reader's emotions.
- Ensure each paragraph advances meaning.
- When uncertain, write shorter.
- Default to plain prose, no headings, no bullet lists. If bullet points requested, keep them tight, no corporate flavor.

## Examples

**Bad (reversal):** "This isn't about money. It's about power."
**Good:** "Money is the instrument. Power is the habit."

**Bad (filler):** "At its core, this is a complex issue. That said, in today's world..."
**Good:** "It is complex. Complexity is not an excuse for fog."
